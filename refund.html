Refund Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Founding Members ($49 one-time)

Changed your mind? You can ask for a full $49 refund until January 15, 2027, for any reason.

Invited members (after Founding spots close)

Monthly or yearly plans can ask for a full refund within 30 days of payment. You can cancel anytime to stop future payments.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email hello@bravvaclub.com from the email you paid with.
  2. Include your PayPal transaction ID (it’s on your PayPal receipt).
  3. We refund to your original PayPal payment, usually within 5 business days.

After a refund

Your membership ends and your Founding number is retired. Founding numbers are never reused.
