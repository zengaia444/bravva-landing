Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Your privacy and safety come first, always. Here is what we collect and why.

When you join

  • From PayPal: your name, email, country and transaction ID. We never see your card or bank details.
  • From you: a different app email, if you send us one.

How we use it

  • To confirm your membership and give you your Founding number.
  • To send your receipt, welcome email and launch updates. You can unsubscribe from updates anytime.
  • To handle refunds and support.

Who helps us

PayPal (payments), Systeme.io (email) and GitHub Pages (this website). They only use your information to provide their service to us. We never sell your data.

In the app

When the app launches, your location is shared only with the safety circle you choose, and Passport pins sit on the city center, never your exact spot. Your journal is private to you. We will publish a full app privacy notice before launch.

Your choices

Email hello@bravvaclub.com to see, correct or delete your information.
