How it works

One minute a day. A circle that has your back.

1

Check in

Unlock with your phone's own fingerprint or passcode, so it's always really you. Share your energy, a gratitude, your top 3 priorities, and your water.

2

Earn points

Every check-in builds your streak and unlocks member perks. Gentle midday nudges keep the habit light, never naggy.

3

Stay seen

Choose a BRAVVA friend as your circle. If you go quiet for 24 hours, she gets a nudge to reach out and check on you.
